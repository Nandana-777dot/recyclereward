↳ Quick Reference

What can you
recycle today?

Always 📦

Paper & Cardboard

Newspapers, boxes, envelopes. Keep dry and flatten before recycling.

Rinse First 🍾

Glass & Plastic

Bottles, jars, containers. Remove lids and rinse clean before sorting.

Crush It 🥫

Metals & Cans

Aluminium cans, tin cans, foil. Crush cans to save space in your bin.

Special Drop 🔌

Electronics

Phones, batteries, cables. Requires special e-waste drop-off. Never bin.
